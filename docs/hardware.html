    CIRCUITS

    ESC signal (D9)

    Servo pulses, 50 per second: 1000 µs = 0 % (stopped), 2000 µs = 100 %. The firmware never goes above MOTOR_MAX_PERCENT = 50 % (1500 µs). The ESC ground must be connected to the Nano GND.

    Battery detection (D6)

    ESC BEC red wire (5 V) --- 10 kΩ --- D6 --- 100 kΩ --- GND

    Battery plugged into the ESC: the BEC gives 5 V and D6 reads HIGH (~4.5 V). Unplugged: the 100 kΩ pulls D6 to LOW. Never connect the BEC to the Nano 5V pin.

    The Nano does not see the battery itself: it sees the BEC 5 V that come through the ESC cable. So ESC NONE means "the ESC is not powered": the battery or the ESC cable was unplugged (tested: both give EVT ESC NONE).

    D6 LOW for more than 50 ms (BAT_DISCONNECT_MS) = battery unplugged. After plugging in, the ESC needs 3 s (BAT_ARM_MS) to arm.

    Battery voltage (A0)

    battery + (balance lead) --- 10 kΩ --- A0 --- 4.7 kΩ --- GND

    V(A0) = V(battery) × 4.7 / (10 + 4.7). A full 3S battery (12.6 V) gives 4.03 V on A0. The highest voltage it can measure is VREF × 14.7 / 4.7 ≈ 14.5 V.

    VREF = 4.64 V: the real voltage on the Nano 5V pin (measured with a multimeter; calibrate it for better readings). Each measurement is the average of 16 readings (BAT_SAMPLES), every 100 ms.

    Push button (D8)

    D8 → button → GND, with the Nano internal pull-up (INPUT_PULLUP): released = HIGH, pressed = LOW. Debounce 20 ms (DEBOUNCE_MS).

    Potentiometer (A6)

    Ends to GND and 5V, wiper to A6. With pin 1 to GND and pin 3 to 5V, turning clockwise increases the value (0..1023). A6 is analog input only on the Nano.

    LEDs (D2, D3, D4)

    Pin → 330 Ω → LED → GND (HIGH = on).
